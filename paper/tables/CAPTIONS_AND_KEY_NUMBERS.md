# Figure captions (numbers filled in from the data; edit the wording freely)

**Figure 1.** Locations of the 514 benchmark intrusions with at least five GEOROC samples, colored by label: fertile (34), weakly mineralized (19) and barren (461). Robinson projection; coordinates are the median of each intrusion's sample coordinates.

**Figure 2.** Intrusion-weighted ROC AUC and 95% bootstrap confidence interval for each of the 12 indices, for all sample types (left) and cumulates only (right). The dashed line marks 0.5 (no skill). Values below 0.5 mean the index ranks barren intrusions above fertile ones. Asterisks mark exploratory indices whose direction was set by the authors; |Δlog(Cu/Pd)| is the absolute departure of log10(Cu/Pd) from the primitive-mantle value.

**Figure 3.** Share of each sample type within fertile, weakly mineralized and barren intrusions (3,047, 1,514 and 8,464 samples). Cumulates make up 65% of fertile samples.

**Figure 4.** AUC by index and sample type. Cells with fewer than five fertile or five barren intrusions with data are left blank and marked "n/a". Diverging scale centered on 0.5. Asterisks as in Figure 2.

# Key numbers (for the text)

- GEOROC analyses screened: 521,961; mafic-ultramafic plutonic samples: 31,886; without an intrusion name: 16,395.
- Benchmark: 13,025 samples, 514 intrusions (34 fertile, 19 weak, 461 barren; 367 barren at LOW confidence).
- Fertile intrusions with chilled-margin samples: 1; with any chilled-margin or fine-grained dike/sill sample: 10.
- Best index: Pd/Ir, AUC 0.75 (95% CI 0.62-0.85); with LOW-confidence barren removed: 0.71.
- Indices with CI entirely above 0.5: pd_ir, pt_pd.
- Indices with CI entirely below 0.5: ni_cu, ni_depletion, cu_zr.
